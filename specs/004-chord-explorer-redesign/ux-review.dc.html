The one-line diagnosis

The widget is built as a configuration tool — pick family, pick key, pick pad count, then read four panels of theory. Your stated job is “hear what a scale sounds like.” Those are different products. Every complaint about “too many controls” resolves the same way: make hearing the default, and make everything else something the user goes looking for.

Nothing below requires deleting features. It requires ranking them — and on mobile, hiding the bottom two thirds of that ranking behind one tap.
